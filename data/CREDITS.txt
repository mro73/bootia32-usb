bootia32.efi
============
Prebuilt IA32 (32-bit) UEFI boot loader for Intel Bay Trail devices, from the
"bootia32-efi" project (https://github.com/lamadotcare/bootia32-efi), which is
released under the Unlicense (public domain).
Upstream sources/credits are listed in that project's README.

SHA-256: 9d3c9683550bbece50432057c0aaa9974a4a705324e8499737613b188c5d1ac0

To use a different build, pick it in the app (the bootia32.efi field) or pass
--efi on the command line.
